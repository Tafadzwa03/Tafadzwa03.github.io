About me

My engineering journey is driven by an interest in reliable, efficient and affordable electrical energy. Growing up in Zimbabwe, where power interruptions are a familiar challenge, inspired my interest in renewable energy and power conversion. I enjoy combining circuit design, simulation, embedded systems and experimental validation to solve practical engineering problems.

Currently, I am pursuing postgraduate studies in power electronics and power systems at IIT Palakkad, India.
